The customer's view

You ask your agent to buy a quiet air purifier. This film is that errand from your side: the limits you set, the tap when a price goes over them, and the receipt at the end.

77 seconds. Captions are in the picture. The narration is a synthetic voice. Same visit, same receipt, as the other three films.

What to notice

  • Before it leaves, you sign a delegation: what it may do, where, and how much it may spend.
  • At the door, Silvector greets your agent and lets it in within those limits.
  • The quietest purifier is $349, over your limit. Nothing happens until you confirm.
